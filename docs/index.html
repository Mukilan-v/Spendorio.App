Spendorio User Manual & Beginner Guide Step-by-Step Instructions

Learn how to manage daily routine tasks, automate expense tracking & monitor your budget

Welcome to Spendorio & SpendTracker!

Spendorio is your personal daily habit manager and automated cost tracker. It links your daily routines directly to your expenses so you never have to enter transactions twice.

1

Enter Account or Guest

Log in with your username (e.g., demo_user) or tap "Continue as Guest User" to safely test all features without modifying authentic records.

100% Offline & Private
2

Check Off Daily Routines

Open the Daily Routines tab or Dashboard. As you finish routines (gym, breakfast, study, PG chores), check the circle to build your consistency streak.

Daily Streak Counter
3

Automated Cost Tracking

Any routine that has a daily cost (e.g. Eggs & Curd: ₹80) automatically logs the expense when checked! No manual math required.

Zero Double Entry
4

Monitor Budget & Savings

Check your real-time Daily and Monthly budget meters. Spendorio alerts you with colors: Green (Safe), Yellow (80% Warning), and Red (Limit Exceeded).

Real-Time KPI Cards
New User Tip: You can use this app on both your laptop (http://localhost:3000) and on your phone browser anywhere on mobile data via Cloudflare tunnel.

Managing Routines & Daily Tasks

Learn how routines, priority levels, time blocks, and automatic cost links work together.

Routine vs One-Time Task

Routine Task: Recurring habits (Gym, Milk, Tech Training, Dinner). Repeats automatically every day.
One-Time Task: Specific single-day item (Doctor visit, Book purchase, Pay college fee).

Setting Linked Task Cost

When adding/editing a task, set the Cost (₹) field (e.g. ₹80 for breakfast). Completing this task automatically creates a matching expense entry tagged "Routine Linked".

Time Blocks & Priority

Organize tasks by Time Blocks: Morning (6am–12pm), Afternoon (12pm–5pm), Evening (5pm–9pm), and Night (9pm+). Filter by High, Medium, or Low priority.

Editing & Deleting Tasks

Click on any task to view its detail modal. Click Edit Task to update the title or time, or Delete to remove the task and its linked expense cleanly.

Tracking Expenses & Incomes

Record standalone purchases, track monthly salary/incomes, and manage your records with instant deletion.

Adding a Standalone Cost

Click the "Log Cost" button in the header or Expenses tab. Enter Title, Amount, Category (Food, Bills, Shopping, etc.), Date, and Payment Method (UPI, Cash, Card, Net Banking).

Logging Incomes & Salary

In the Cost & Expenses tab, click "Log Income" to record monthly salary, freelancing, or allowances. Net Balance updates immediately: (Income − Expense).

Deleting Records Permanently

Click the red 🗑️ Delete icon next to any expense or income record. Click OK to confirm. The item is permanently removed and will never reappear upon refresh or server sync.

Filtering & Searching

Use the Search bar to instantly filter by expense title (e.g. "Cylinder", "PG", "Snacks"), or filter by Category and Routine-Linked status.

Budget Limits & Overspend Warnings

Set spending boundaries so you never run out of money before month-end.

Download your data as an Excel-ready CSV Spreadsheet or export a Full JSON Backup in Settings & Backup anytime for offline preservation.

Pro Tips & Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about using Spendorio.

Q: Do I need any special software or terminal open to use Spendorio?

No! Spendorio runs 100% independently in any browser as an offline-first Progressive Web App.

Q: How do I start or stop Spendorio manually?

Use the colorful desktop shortcuts: double-click Spendorio & SpendTracker to launch, or double-click Stop Spendorio to cleanly shut down.

Q: How do I switch between Dark and Light mode?

Click the moon/sun icon in the top header bar (or in Settings) to toggle between modern Instagram Dark mode and clean Light mode.

Free Google Firebase Setup

Connect your laptop and phone in 3 easy steps (Takes 2 minutes, 100% Free):

1
Go to console.firebase.google.com and click "Create a project" (Name it Spendorio).
2
In the left sidebar under Build, click Realtime Database > click Create Database (Choose default location and select Start in test mode).
3
Copy the Database URL at the top (e.g. https://spendorio-app-default-rtdb.firebaseio.com) and paste it into the box in Settings!

✨ Once connected: Any task or cost you add on your phone will automatically appear on your laptop, and vice versa!
